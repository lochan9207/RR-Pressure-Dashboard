The scouting method

I

Define pressure

A gradient-boosted model estimates the batting side’s win chance before every ball. Leverage is how much the next ball can move it. The top quarter in each innings is high pressure.

II

Score every ball

Each ball is compared with the league average for the same innings, over and wickets. Wickets become runs, and opposition strength is adjusted for.

III

Rate and rank

E = s + (p − s) × N / (N + 500) blends pressure form (p) with overall skill (s). A consistency score adds 20% of the final result.

How far to trust it

Honest read: raw pressure-only performance barely repeats between two halves of the data, so the rating leans on overall skill. The batting order below the top three is close, and the bowling list is best read as a shortlist of strong, pressure-tested bowlers.
