How I defined pressure

A gradient-boosted model estimates the batting side’s chance of winning before every ball. Leverage is the expected size of the swing in that chance from the next ball across all outcomes. High pressure means the top quartile of leverage within each innings, so first-innings collapses count too.

How the rating works

Each player’s pressure record is blended with his overall skill: E = s + (p − s) × N / (N + 500), where N is his number of pressure balls. A consistency score (share of pressure matches with positive value) adds 20% of the final score.

How far to trust it

Raw pressure-only performance barely repeats between two halves of the data, so the rating leans on overall skill. The batting order below the top three is close, and the bowling list is best read as a shortlist of strong, pressure-tested bowlers.
